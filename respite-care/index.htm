Livewell with Respite Care

Respite care is a term used to describe a period of dedicated relief for caregivers. Caring for a person with dementia can be extremely demanding. The aim of respite care is to provide some relief for carers and support them in having a break from their caring responsibilities, to look after their own health and wellbeing. A short-term care period for your loved one when you need a break to look after your own needs.

Over and above caring for our permanent residents, Livewell offers those suffering from dementia the opportunity to join us as day- or short-term visitors. To benefit from all our specialist services and staff, including a personalised meal plan, participating in our holistic and rewarding memory-care activity programmes within a safe and serene environment and a private, luxury room – all with the care of our dedicated, professional staff.

Our short-term respite care has been designed to enable caregivers to take a break from their responsibilities for a few days, or an extended period with complete peace of mind. 

Get in touch with our team to discuss your requirements and create a specialised respite care programme for your loved one.

Respite Care Services

During your loved one’s stay, they will benefit from our permanent resident offering. Services may also be customised according to your loved one’s special needs. The care service offering includes:

  • Activities of Daily Living Support. Self-care assistance for bathing, dressing, mobility, lavatory.
  • A daily activity programme with many activities and exercises to choose from.
  • Nutritious meals and snacks.
  • Medical and health services, if required.
  • Medical specialists and support on stand-by.
  • Social interaction and stimulation with other guests.
  • A luxury, private room.
  • Round-the-clock care.
  • A safe environment with free, supervised movement.
  • Support services by our team for family members.

Respite care offers the perfect solution for caregivers with daytime commitments, that need to go away for a period of time, or if you are considering a trial-stay at our facility.

Respite care enables families and carers to have a rest, go out, attend to business or go on a holiday.
